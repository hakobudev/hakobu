Every connection starts from your server

It dials out to the panel and to Cloudflare, and visitors reach your apps through a Cloudflare Tunnel with HTTPS on your domains. No public IP, no ports to open, no SSH keys handed to anyone.

Backups only your server can open

Databases and volumes go to R2 in your Cloudflare account every day, sealed with the server's own key, and each is restored into a test database right after upload.

Errors, logs and traces

Any Sentry SDK works: hakobu sets SENTRY_DSN and keeps what your apps send on the server they run on.

Or tell Claude to deploy it

hakobu is an MCP server for claude.ai, the Claude apps and Claude Code. Claude reads your apps' logs and errors, pushes a fix and checks it landed. It deploys only if you let it, and never deletes anything or sees a secret.

Use ours, or run your own.

The same binary either way, and either way your apps and their data stay on your servers and in your Cloudflare account. Bring a Linux server and a domain on Cloudflare; the free plan is enough.

Hosted panel

Sign in at dash.hakobu.dev and add your servers with one command each.

  • The panel runs no apps of its own
  • It can't open your backups: only your servers can
  • Your apps' logs and errors stay on your servers

Self-hosted

Install the panel on your own server. It asks for a Cloudflare token and a subdomain, then gives you a link to claim it.

$ curl -fsSL https://hakobu.dev/install.sh | sudo bash

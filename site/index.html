Where everything lives.

The panel gives the orders, whether it's ours at dash.hakobu.dev or your own. Your apps, their data and their traffic stay with you.

Yours.

On your servers and in your Cloudflare account.

  • Your apps and their databases
  • Logs, errors and traces, sealed
  • The only key to the backups
  • Backups in R2, sealed
  • The tunnel your visitors use
  • DNS records for your domains

The panel.

Ours, or one you run yourself.

  • Projects and settings
  • Variables, encrypted

Never: your visitors' traffic, your apps' data, or the key to your backups.

I build hakobu on my own, and it runs my apps and a client's today. Every feature on this page is one I needed on a real server first.
